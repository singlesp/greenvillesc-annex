How would annexation into the City of Greenville change your yearly costs?

If your property is in unincorporated Greenville County, joining the city changes more than one line on your tax bill. Some costs go up, some go down, and some stay the same. This calculator adds them all up for your property.

It is an independent, unofficial estimate. It is not run by the City of Greenville or Greenville County. The address or parcel number you search for is sent to Greenville County’s public GIS service to find the record; everything else you enter stays in your browser.
